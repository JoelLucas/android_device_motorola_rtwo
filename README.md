The Motorola Edge 40 Pro (Moto X40 in China and Edge+ 2023 in North America) is Motorola's flagship smartphone powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 platform.

Announced in April 2023, the device combines flagship performance, premium materials, advanced camera capabilities, ultra-fast charging technologies, and one of the fastest smartphone displays available, featuring a 165Hz curved pOLED panel. It was designed to deliver top-tier performance for gaming, multitasking, photography, and everyday use while maintaining a slim and elegant design.
